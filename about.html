01The short version

MSc in Computer Science and Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, then seven years of building data and AI systems. I started on real-time ML for factory assembly lines and backend systems for financial services; I grew into leading an AI engineering team of five, shipping LLM products for enterprise clients; today I lead AI adoption for a consulting firm, replacing manual processes with systems that can be audited.

02How I work

Process before technology. I map the real workflow — handoffs, exceptions, approvals — before designing anything. A pilot on the wrong process fails even with the right model.

Evidence over confidence. Every output must carry a verifiable source. Where the system is unsure, it says so and passes the decision to a human.

Ship, then tighten. A working automation today beats a perfect one next month — but evals and QA come before it touches live data, never after.

Handoffs close the loop. A deployment is done when the customer's own team is trained, documented and independent.

03Principles
Auditability
Auditability first. If an output can't be checked against its source, it doesn't ship.
Determinism
Deterministic where possible, agentic where needed. Code decides what can be decided by code; the model handles judgement, and never the final status.
Humans
Humans decide. Models propose, code verifies, people sign off.
Measurement
Measure what matters. Hours reclaimed, coverage, error rates — reported, not assumed.
Configuration
Configuration over code. A new use case should be a new rule set, not a new platform.
