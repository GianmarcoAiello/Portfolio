01Context

Process and document knowledge is scattered: some of it in shared folders, much of it in employees' heads and on their PCs. Manual, repetitive work depends on the few people who know how it is done; newcomers reconstruct it by asking around. And AI assistants with no governance would access that knowledge without control or trail.

02Approach
  • Collect: document and process knowledge is consolidated into two Git repositories — a curated repo (processes, rules, skills) and a document repo. Git is the system of record.
  • Connect: the MCP layer is the connector between AI harnesses and that data — search, read, propose. One open standard: any MCP-compatible harness works, no custom plug-in per client.
  • Execute: harnesses like Claude Code accomplish custom tasks and workflows. Crafted skills make them repeatable and usable by anyone in the company, newcomers included — people interact with Git in natural language, with no tech knowledge.
  • Monitor: every access recorded; AI gateway (e.g. LiteLLM) for model routing, budgets and central usage data; evals measure quality over time.
  • Improve safely: governed contribution — propose in natural language → validate → pull request → human review → merge. No direct writes, even for trusted builders: every change has an author, a diff and a reviewer.

03What we expect to measure
  • Repeatable workflows that previously lived in one person's head or PC, usable by anyone in the company — newcomers included.
  • Reduced manual and repetitive workload: time freed for higher-value tasks.
  • Capacity to scale: freed time goes into growth and new scenarios, not into more of the same manual work.
  • Faster onboarding: the process knowledge a newcomer needs is written down, executable and reachable through one assistant.
  • Higher deliverable quality through verifiable checks and a review trail.

In production; numbers to be published when the measurement window closes.

Lesson
The hard part is not the AI — it is making knowledge writable safely. Crafted skills and governed contribution turn individual habits into company capability; retrieval alone would just make the old chaos faster.
